High entropy alloys (HEAs) are a versatile class of electrocatalysts with tunable surface properties,
compositions, and synergistic elemental effects, notably enhancing the hydrogen evolution reaction
(HER).Herein,wereportthedevelopmentofPdIrSnZnMoHEAswithvaryingPdloadings(0.01–0.05M)
using a facile solvothermal method. The structural features and composition of the prepared
PdIrSnZnMo HEA nanostructures were characterized using X-ray diffraction (XRD), X-ray photoelectron
spectroscopy (XPS), field emission scanning electron microscopy (FESEM), and high-resolution
transmission electron microscopy (HR-TEM). Systematic electrocatalytic studies revealed a remarkable
HER performance even at a low Pd loading (0.01 M). Notably, the PdIrSnZnMo HEA with 0.05 M Pd
exhibitedanultra-lowoverpotentialof17mVtoachieveacurrentdensityof−10mAcm−2inanacidic
medium, surpassing the conventional Pt/C electrocatalyst. The electrocatalyst also demonstrated
outstanding durability and maintained its stability without decomposition during prolonged operation.
