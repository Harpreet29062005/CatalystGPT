ABSTRACT: Photocatalytic reduction of nitrite (NO2 −) to ammonia (NH3) presents a sustainable path for wastewater remediation and

heterointerfaces. The unique interfacial architecture synergistically optimizes the bidentate adsorption of nitrite and accelerates water

dynamicsforadvancedenergyandenvironmentalcatalysis.

2.1SynthesisofTNS s
TiO nanosheets with exposed {001} facets (denoted as TNS) were synthesized via a modified hydrothermal method adapted from a
2
previouslyreportedprotocol[1].Inatypical procedure, 25mLoftetrabutyl titanatewasmixed with3.0mL ofhydrofluoricacid (40
u
wt%, serving as a morphology-directing agent) under vigorous stirring to form a homogeneous precursor solution. The resulting
mixture was thentransferred intoa Teflon-lined stainless-steel autoclave and maintained at 200 °C for 24 h.After thehydrothermal
reaction, the white pJrecipitate was collected by centrifugation, washed sequentially with deionized water and ethanol to remove
