HEA catalysts offer a broad design space,[9] while effi-
cient identification of the optimal alloy combination remains
a formidable challenge. Traditional screening methods typi-
cally rely on extensive experiment and computation, which are
time- and resource-consuming.[10] Moreover, the synthesis of
HEA catalysts is often complex, with sintering processes re-
quiring extended durations, further slowing down the devel-
opment of catalysts.[11,12] To address these challenges, artifi-
cial intelligence[13,14] (AI) and machine learning[15] (ML) have
been applied to catalyst design and optimization in recent
years, achieving significant progress. By simulating the pro-
cess of natural selection and genetic variation, genetic algo-
rithmscanefficientlyexplorecomplexHEAcatalystsandavoid
falling into local optimal solutions. Meanwhile, literature min-
ing techniques based on LLMs[16,17] can automatically identify
keycatalystelementsrelatedtocatalyticperformancefromexten-
sivescientificliteratures,providingpreciseguidanceforcatalyst
design.
